# FieldBridge Demo Script

Target: 3–4 minutes, one continuous live take after AWS deployment is verified.

1. **Problem, 20 seconds** — A field-service coordinator receives a thin ticket
   and must reconcile contract rules, missing evidence, replacement constraints,
   and ownership before promising anything.
2. **Safety boundary, 15 seconds** — Show `GET /api/v1/boundary`: FieldBridge can
   inspect, recommend, and draft; it cannot dispatch, order parts, contact a
   customer, or close a ticket.
3. **Core workflow, 100 seconds** — Submit the synthetic Civic Research
   replacement case. Show the P2 deadline, missing actual-user contact, chaperone
   constraint, replacement-IP registration, managed-workflow verification, and
   draft customer-update requirement.
4. **Platform-routing edge case, 40 seconds** — Submit an enterprise-fax fixture
   without a printer serial. Show internal platform ownership rather than a false
   local-IT redirect or invented serial requirement.
5. **Strands proof, 40 seconds** — Show the agent selecting the bounded policy,
   routing, handoff, and safety tools. Show its response citing the deterministic
   decision and review reasons.
6. **Engineering proof, 25 seconds** — Show tests, coverage, architecture, and
   the deployed AWS runtime. State exactly what is synthetic and what is not yet
   a production integration.

Do not show real tickets, names, systems, customer terms, contacts, credentials,
or inventory data.
