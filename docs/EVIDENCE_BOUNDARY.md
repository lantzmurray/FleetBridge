# FieldBridge Evidence Boundary

## Allowed source material

- The builder's generalized field-service and support experience.
- Original synthetic contracts, ticket fixtures, serial values, system labels,
  policies, and scenarios.
- Public Strands and AWS documentation when cited in future deployment material.

## Never add

- Employer or customer names, ticket text, logs, contacts, emails, serials,
  locations, credentials, screenshots, or unapproved manuals.
- Actual pricing, service-level agreements, dispatch policies, inventory data, or
  details that identify a customer contract.
- Integrations that can dispatch work, order parts, contact a customer, update a
  ticket, or close a ticket without an explicitly designed and authenticated
  production approval boundary.

## Claim discipline

- "Inspired by field-service operations experience" is acceptable.
- "Used at", "deployed for", "integrated with", or "reduced time for" a real
  employer or customer is not supported by this repository and must not be
  claimed.
- The local API and tests demonstrate synthetic, draft-only behavior. They do
  not demonstrate Bedrock invocation, AgentCore deployment, production tenancy,
  authentication, or integration with ticketing/dispatch systems.
